06 · Impact Assessment

Don't confuse
output with impact.

Sebuah program dapat menghasilkan output yang besar, tetapi dampak jangka panjang harus dibuktikan dengan indikator outcome dan impact.

01 · INPUT

Resources

Dana, manusia, teknologi, fasilitas, energi, kemitraan, dan sumber daya lain.

02 · OUTPUT

Activities

Contoh: jumlah bibit ditanam, volume sampah dikelola, kapasitas solar PV.

03 · OUTCOME

Change

Perubahan perilaku, kualitas lingkungan, pengurangan risiko, atau efisiensi.

04 · IMPACT

Long-term

Perubahan lingkungan dan sosial yang bertahan dalam jangka panjang.

Critical lens: 173.000 bibit mangrove adalah output. Untuk menyebutnya sebagai impact, diperlukan bukti seperti survival rate, perubahan kondisi ekosistem, kualitas pesisir, dan manfaat jangka panjang.

11 · Critical Review

What looks good.
What still needs work.

✓ Positive Signals

Real actions exist.

  • Current study · Renewable energy reached 54%.
  • Current study · Recycled fiber reached 52%.
  • B3 waste decreased substantially from the 2018 baseline.
  • Solar PV capacity increased from 21.5 MWp to 32 MWp.
  • Biodiversity partnerships are multi-year.
  • External validation such as PROPER strengthens credibility.
! Still Needs Work

Impact is not yet optimal.

  • Carbon intensity increased from 1.08 to 1.17.
  • SO₂ increased from 141.8 to 230.02 mg/Nm³.
  • COD increased from 137.8 to 150.97 mg/l.
  • Carbon, energy, and water reduction targets remain challenging.
  • One administrative sanction was disclosed for Indah Kiat Serang.
  • Public complaint data lacks longer-term trend context.
12 · Our Verdict

REAL ACTIONS EXIST.
BUT THE IMPACT
IS NOT YET OPTIMAL.

Berdasarkan assessment kami, CSR lingkungan perusahaan menunjukkan tindakan nyata dan beberapa progres terukur. Namun, peningkatan pada sejumlah indikator lingkungan menunjukkan bahwa program tersebut belum dapat dianggap menyelesaikan seluruh akar masalah operasional.

CSR: Cukup Efektif Sustainability: Needs Improvement Greenwashing: Not Proven Consistency: Further Review
